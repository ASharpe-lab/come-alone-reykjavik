# AI Usage Report

## Agreed AI usage plan

AI may help the team:

- draft and improve interview questions;
- translate and organise real interview notes;
- identify possible themes for the team to verify;
- explain code and suggest implementation approaches;
- assist with documentation and testing.

AI must not:

- invent participants, interviews, answers, quotes, events or users;
- present assumptions as research findings;
- replace interviews or usability testing;
- make final product decisions without team review.

All AI output must be reviewed and verified by a team member.
